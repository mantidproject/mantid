- In the new Instrument View, the ``Settings`` tab now has ``Rotate 180°`` and ``Rotate`` controls for the flat projections. These turn the projection about its axis, moving the seam at which the instrument is cut open and unwrapped, so a bank that is split across the left and right edges can be brought back together in the middle. ``Rotate 180°`` applies the half turn that is most often wanted, and the slider allows any other angle. They are the equivalent of the old Instrument View's ``U Correction``, and unlike ``Flip Beam`` they leave the order of the banks unchanged.
