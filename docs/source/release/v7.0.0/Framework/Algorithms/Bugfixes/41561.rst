- :ref:`algm-AbsorptionCorrection` now applies the sample orientation. It integrates over the sample shape attached to the workspace, and where the shape was left in the sample's own frame - which is what :ref:`algm-SetGoniometer` on its own does - the goniometer rotation was silently ignored, so the gauge volume was intersected with the sample in the wrong place and the paths through it were traced through the wrong geometry. The rotation the shape already carries is now taken into account, so a sample oriented with :ref:`algm-SetGoniometer` and one oriented by :ref:`algm-CopySample` onto an already-oriented workspace give the same answer, matching :ref:`algm-EstimateScatteringVolumeCentreOfMass`. Results change for any rotated sample: for a 20 mm gauge volume straddling the face of a rotated 40 mm cuboid the correction factors move by a factor of four. Workspaces without a goniometer are unaffected. The other algorithms in this family are unaffected, since they build their sample shape from their own dimensions rather than reading it from the workspace.
